Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you still have concerns, feel free to contact our support team.

What are the requirements to apply?

To apply for a loan with Quick Duit, you need to be at least 18 years old, possess a valid government-issued ID, provide proof of a steady income, and have an active bank account in your name.

How long does the approval process take?

Our automated system is designed for speed. In most cases, you will receive a decision within a few hours of submitting your fully completed application and required documents.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

No, we pride ourselves on absolute transparency. All interest rates, processing fees, and repayment terms are clearly outlined in your loan offer before you sign anything.
